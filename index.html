About DebtQuest

DebtQuest is an iPhone app that helps you record debts, track repayments and celebrate your progress through XP, ranks and achievements.

DebtQuest is a tracking and motivational tool. It does not provide financial, legal or investment advice.

Privacy Policy

Effective: 26 September 2026

Your privacy

DebtQuest stores your app information locally on your device. Your debt records, receipt photos and scanned receipt text are not sent to us or to an external processing service. We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

Information you enter

Quest names, balances, currencies, interest rates, repayments, debt additions and notes are stored locally on your device. DebtQuest does not operate servers that store these records.

Receipt photos and scanning

You can optionally attach a receipt by taking a photo or selecting an image from Photos. DebtQuest stores a compressed copy locally as part of the entry.

Receipt scanning uses Apple’s on-device text recognition to suggest possible amounts. Receipt images and recognised text are not uploaded to an external scanning or AI service. You choose and check the amount before recording it.

Camera and Photos permissions

DebtQuest requests camera access when you choose to take a photo. When choosing from Photos, you select the image you want to attach using the system photo picker.

“Save to Photos” requests permission to add a separate receipt copy to your photo library. Photos taken within DebtQuest are not automatically saved there. You can manage camera and Photos permissions in your device’s Settings.

Storage and backups

DebtQuest does not provide its own cloud storage or synchronisation. App data may be included in backups managed by your device or Apple account, depending on your settings. Images saved to Photos are subject to your Photos and iCloud settings.

Analytics and advertising

DebtQuest does not use third-party analytics, advertising trackers or advertising networks.

Accounts and external services

DebtQuest does not require an account and does not connect to bank accounts or other financial services.

Deleting your information

You can remove receipt attachments and delete records inside the app. Deleting DebtQuest from your device removes its locally stored app data, subject to any backups managed through your device or Apple account.

Removing a receipt from DebtQuest does not delete the original image or any copies saved to Photos. You can delete those separately in Photos and manage backups through your device or Apple account.

Support enquiries

If you email support, we receive your email address and any information or attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond to your enquiry. Please avoid sending sensitive financial details or unredacted receipts.

Children’s privacy

DebtQuest is not directed specifically at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if DebtQuest’s features or privacy practices change. The effective date above will show when the latest version took effect.
